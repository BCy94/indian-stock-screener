Our Story

Investing education,
minus the hype

Why "So Called Investor"? Because real investing isn't about predictions or paid pumps — it's about discipline, frameworks and honesty about what we don't know.

Mission

Teaching people to think like investors, not gamblers

So Called Investor started as a personal blog documenting real portfolio decisions, mistakes and lessons in the Indian markets. It grew into a place where thousands of readers came for the same thing: practical, no-nonsense guidance you could actually act on.

Today, that same philosophy drives every course and resource here — built on frameworks tested with real money, not theory borrowed from a textbook.

Journey

From a personal blog to a learning platform

2014

First investment, first mistakes

Started investing with no framework — learned the hard way why most beginners lose money chasing tips.

2018

So Called Investor blog launched

Began documenting real portfolio analysis and lessons publicly, building a community of honest, curious investors.

2023

Free materials & tools library

Released checklists, trackers and the free Nifty stock screener tool used by thousands every month.

2026

Moving to a dedicated platform

Rebuilding everything into a premium, easy-to-use home for courses, materials and community — this site.
